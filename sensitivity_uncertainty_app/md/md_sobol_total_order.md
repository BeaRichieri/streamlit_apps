The total-order index includes the individual effect of parameter $i$ **and
every interaction involving that parameter**.

For example, with three parameters:

$$
S_{T_1}
=
\frac{
V_1+V_{12}+V_{13}+V_{123}
}{
\mathrm{Var}(Y)
}.
$$

So $S_{T_i}$ answers: **how much of the output variance is associated with
parameter $i$, either alone or through interactions?**
