The two bars answer complementary questions:

- **First-order $S_i$:** contribution of parameter $i$ acting alone.
- **Total-order $S_{T_i}$:** contribution of parameter $i$ acting alone **plus
  every interaction involving it**.

Therefore:

- if $S_i$ and $S_{T_i}$ are nearly equal, interactions involving the parameter
  are weak;
- if $S_{T_i}$ is clearly larger than $S_i$, interactions are important.
- $$ 0\le S_i\le S_{T_i}\le 1 $$
