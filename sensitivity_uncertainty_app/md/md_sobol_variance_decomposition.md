For three parameters, Sobol' decomposes the total output variance into

$$
\mathrm{Var}(Y)
=
V_1+V_2+V_3
+V_{12}+V_{13}+V_{23}
+V_{123}.
$$

Here, $V_i$ is the variance contribution of parameter $i$ acting alone,
while terms such as $V_{13}$ represent interactions.

The sensitivity indices are therefore **fractions of the total output
variance**.
