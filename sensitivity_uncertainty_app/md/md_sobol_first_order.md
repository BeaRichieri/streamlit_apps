The first-order index is the fraction of the total output variance associated
with parameter $i$ **acting alone**:

$$
S_i=\frac{V_i}{\mathrm{Var}(Y)}.
$$

For example, $S_i=0.40$ means that approximately **40% of the output variance**
is attributed to the individual effect of parameter $i$.

The first-order index does **not** include interactions with other parameters.
