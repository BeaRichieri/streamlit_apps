We use the same simple reservoir that will later be used in the GLUE page. The precipitation forcing and initial storage are fixed, while two parameters control the reservoir response:

- $k$: reservoir discharge coefficient;
- $b$: nonlinearity exponent.

The purpose here is **not yet to sample an ensemble or define behavioural simulations**. Instead, we compare parameter combinations directly and ask whether different values of $k$ and $b$ can reproduce the same observations similarly well.
