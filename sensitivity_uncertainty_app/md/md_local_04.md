The synthetic response of $x_3$ is strongly nonlinear and non-monotonic. Around $x_3=0.5$, however, the response curve is locally flat and the derivative is approximately zero.

A purely local analysis at that point can therefore classify $x_3$ as locally unimportant even though changing $x_3$ over its full range produces a large change in the model output.

This motivates the transition to **global sensitivity analysis**.
