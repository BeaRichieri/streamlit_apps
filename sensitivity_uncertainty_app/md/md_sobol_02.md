Suppose the model output is

$$
Y=f(X_1,X_2,\ldots,X_k),
$$

where the parameters $X_1,\ldots,X_k$ are uncertain and vary over predefined
ranges.

Sobol' analysis decomposes the variance of $Y$ into contributions associated
with the individual parameters and their interactions.

Two indices are especially useful: the **first-order index** $S_i$ and the
**total-order index** $S_{T_i}$.
