🧭 **Try it yourself**

- Move the reference value of $x_1$ and observe how its local sensitivity changes.
- Set $x_3=0.5$. What happens to its local sensitivity?
- Then move $x_3$ away from 0.5. Does the parameter remain locally insensitive?
- Change the perturbation size $\Delta x$. Are the calculated sensitivities identical?

The important point is that **local sensitivity depends on where the model is evaluated**.
