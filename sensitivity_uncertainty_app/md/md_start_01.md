This app provides a structured introduction to **sensitivity analysis** and **parameter uncertainty analysis** through a sequence of interactive methods.

### Sensitivity analysis
The sensitivity section progresses from **local sensitivity analysis** to the global **Morris screening method** and finally to **Sobol' variance-based sensitivity analysis**.

### Parameter uncertainty
The uncertainty section introduces parameter uncertainty and equifinality, then demonstrates **GLUE — Generalised Likelihood Uncertainty Estimation**.

The aim is to develop an intuitive understanding of how these methods are constructed, what questions they answer, and how parameter uncertainty propagates into model predictions.
