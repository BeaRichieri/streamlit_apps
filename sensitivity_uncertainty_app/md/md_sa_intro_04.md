### Local sensitivity analysis

A local sensitivity analysis evaluates model response close to **one reference parameter set**. Parameters are usually perturbed one at a time while the remaining parameters are kept fixed.

**Strengths**

- computationally inexpensive;
- intuitive;
- useful close to a calibrated or reference solution.

**Limitations**

- results depend on the selected reference point;
- sensitivities elsewhere in the parameter space may be missed;
- nonlinearities and interactions are difficult to characterize globally.
