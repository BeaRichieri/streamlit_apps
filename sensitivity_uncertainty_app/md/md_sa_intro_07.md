### Variance-based quantification — Sobol'

Sobol' analysis quantifies how much of the **variance of the model output** can be attributed to individual parameters and their interactions.

The most commonly interpreted indices are:

- **first-order index $S_i$** — contribution of parameter $i$ alone;
- **total-order index $S_{T_i}$** — contribution of parameter $i$ including all interactions involving that parameter.
