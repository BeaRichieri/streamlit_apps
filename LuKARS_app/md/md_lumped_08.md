A semi-distributed model divides the catchment into several **hydrological response units**. Each unit represents an area expected to respond similarly because of shared characteristics such as soil, land use, geology, or topography.

The units are simulated separately and their contributions are subsequently combined to obtain the overall catchment response.

**LuKARS** is a semi-distributed conceptual model. It divides a karst catchment into hydrotopes with distinct hydrological behaviour.
