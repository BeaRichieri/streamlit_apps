The **matrix-conduit exchange flux** $Q_{\mathrm{MC},t}$ (m³/$\Delta t$) depends on the difference between the dimensionless water levels in the matrix $M_t$ and conduit $C_t$.

The sign function allows the exchange direction to change. Water can move from the matrix toward the conduit or from the conduit back toward the matrix. Positive values represent transfer from matrix to conduit; negative values represent transfer in the opposite direction.

**Symbols in this equation**

| Symbol | Unit | Interpretation |
|---|---:|---|
| $R_a$ | m² | Total recharge area. |
| 🔵 $k_{\mathrm{MC}}$ | mm/$\Delta t$ | Discharge coefficient controlling the strength of exchange between matrix and conduit. |
| $M_t$ | – | Dimensionless water level in the matrix. |
| $C_t$ | – | Dimensionless water level in the conduit. |
| 🔵 $a_{\mathrm{MC}}$ | – | Dimensionless exponent controlling the nonlinearity of matrix-conduit exchange. |
