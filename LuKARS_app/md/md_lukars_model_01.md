# LuKARS model description

LuKARS is a **semi-distributed hydrological conceptual model** designed to represent lumped recharge, storage, and discharge processes in karst systems.

LuKARS was first developed by Bittner et al. (2018) to account for land use changes, and further developed and tested in the successive years (Sivelle et al., 2022; Richieri et al., 2024; Richieri et al., 2025).
 
The model combines spatially differentiated upper compartments, which represnet the epikarst, with conceptual matrix and conduit compartments representing the dual behaviour of kasrst systems.
