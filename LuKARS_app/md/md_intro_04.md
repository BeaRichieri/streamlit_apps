Karst is a landscape and hydrological system characterized by the **dissolution of soluble rocks** and the development of features such as caves, sinkholes, enlarged fractures, and underground conduits. Karst systems are most commonly associated with carbonate rocks, particularly limestone and dolostone. Their strongly **heterogeneous structure** creates a complex hydrological behaviour, with water moving through both fast preferential pathways such as conduits and enlarged fractures and slower pathways through the rock matrix and less permeable fractures.
