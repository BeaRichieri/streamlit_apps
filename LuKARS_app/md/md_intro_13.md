| **Autogenic recharge** | **Allogenic recharge** |
|---|---|
| Recharge generated **within** the karst catchment itself: diffuse infiltration through soil and epikarst; internal runoff over exposed carbonate rock; local infiltration through fractures and dolines | Recharge originating **outside** the karst area and subsequently entering the karst aquifer, commonly through external runoff, sinking streams, or concentrated inflow through swallow holes |

Diffuse recharge generally enters the system over a broad area. Concentrated recharge can deliver large volumes of water rapidly to fractures and the conduit network.
