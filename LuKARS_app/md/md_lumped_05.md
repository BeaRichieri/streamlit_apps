| Characteristic | Distributed models | Conceptual models |
|---|---|---|
| Spatial representation | Explicit two- or three-dimensional discretization | No complete spatial discretization |
| Main representation | Groundwater heads and spatial flow fields | Storages and fluxes between conceptual compartments |
| Data requirements | Generally high | Generally lower |
| Computational demand | Usually high | Low to moderate |
| Parameters | Often linked to measurable aquifer properties | Usually effective parameters estimated through calibration |
| Typical outputs | Spatial and temporal groundwater-head distributions | Spring discharge, storage, recharge, and internal fluxes |
| Main advantage | Detailed spatial representation | Simplicity and computational efficiency |
| Main limitation | High data and computational requirements | Limited explicit spatial information |
