They are particularly useful because:

- karst spring discharge integrates the response of a large part of the aquifer;
- precipitation, evapotranspiration, and discharge time series are often more readily available than detailed subsurface information;
- rapid model execution supports calibration, sensitivity analysis, and uncertainty analysis;
- different hypotheses about recharge, storage, and internal flow can be tested efficiently;
- the models can support scenario analysis and hydrological forecasting.
