The **Baget karst system** is located in the French Pyrenees, approximately 10 km southwest of Saint-Girons.

The catchment has a recharge area of about 13 $km^2$ and is characterized by a heterogeneous geology dominated by limestone, with black flysch covering approximately 30% of the catchment.

Groundwater mainly discharges at the perennial Las Hountas spring. During high-flow events, the normally dry Lachein stream can become active and bypass part of the spring discharge.
