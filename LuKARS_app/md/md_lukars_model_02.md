The recharge area is divided into a **user-defined number of hydrotopes**. Each hydrotope represents an area with characteristic hydrological behaviour: different land use, soil type and/or thickness, and geological characteristics.

Hydrotopes are independent non-linear units, representing the response of the epikarst zone.

The water stored in each hydrotope can follow a **slower pathway toward the matrix (Qis)** or a **faster pathway toward the conduit system (Qhyd)**. Each hydrotope accounts for the share of water that is drained out of the spring catchment (Qsec). The lower model structure represents storage, **exchange (QMC)**, and **discharge from the matrix (QMS) and conduit (QCS) domains to the spring**.
