- They do not explicitly resolve the complete spatial distribution of aquifer properties.
- Storages or **buckets** represent relevant parts of the hydrological system.
- **Transfer functions** describe water entering, leaving, or moving between storages.
- They are relatively easy to implement and computationally efficient.
- They can be useful where detailed spatial information is unavailable.
- Their short run times facilitate calibration, sensitivity analysis, and uncertainty quantification.

Their parameters are generally **effective parameters** that represent system behaviour and need to be estimated through calibration (cannot be measured in the field).
