**Sensitivity analysis (SA)** investigates how variation in model parameters is associated with variation in a selected model response.

In hydrology, sensitivity analysis is commonly used to:

- identify parameters that strongly control a model output;
- identify parameters that have little influence within the investigated ranges;
- improve understanding of how model processes generate the simulated response;
- reduce the dimensionality of a later calibration or uncertainty analysis;
- prioritize field measurements or parameter-characterisation efforts.

Sensitivity analysis does **not** by itself quantify the full uncertainty of a prediction. It answers a related but different question: **which uncertain inputs matter for the response that we are analysing?**
