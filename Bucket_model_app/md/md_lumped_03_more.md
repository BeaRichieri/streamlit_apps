- The aquifer is divided into a two- or three-dimensional numerical grid.
- Parameters and state variables are assigned to individual grid cells.
- Governing flow equations are solved throughout the model domain.
- The result can describe the spatial and temporal evolution of groundwater heads and fluxes.
- Detailed information about geology, hydraulic properties, and boundary conditions is generally required.
- Model construction and simulation can be computationally demanding.

Groundwater flow through the rock matrix is commonly represented using equations based on **Darcy's law**. Additional formulations may be required to reproduce rapid flow through fractures and conduits.
