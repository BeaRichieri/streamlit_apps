The predefined dataset contains daily model forcing and spring-discharge observations.

- **S0** is used as the source/sink forcing for Hydrotope 1.
- **S123** is used for Hydrotopes 2 and 3.
- **QobsS** contains observed spring discharge and is converted from L/s to m³/s before comparison with the simulation.
- **Q_sim_Bittner2018**, where available, can optionally be shown as a reference series in the calibration plot.
