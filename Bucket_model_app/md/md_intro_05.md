Limestone consists mainly of calcite ($\mathrm{CaCO_3}$), whereas dolostone consists mainly of dolomite ($\mathrm{CaMg(CO_3)_2}$).

Karst can also develop in other soluble rocks, including gypsum and halite. However, carbonate sedimentary rocks are the most important karst-forming rocks for groundwater-resource management.

Karst landscapes are characterised by distinctive surface and subsurface features, including karren, dolines, sinkholes, dry valleys, caves, conduits, and large springs. These features develop gradually through water–rock interaction over long periods.

> **Key message**  
> Karst systems have a highly heterogeneous internal structure that is often difficult to observe directly.
