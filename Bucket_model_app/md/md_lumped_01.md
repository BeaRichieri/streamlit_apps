# Lumped conceptual models

Hydrological models are simplified representations of the processes governing the storage, movement, and distribution of water within the hydrological cycle.

They help us investigate how a hydrological system responds to inputs such as precipitation and snowmelt, losses such as evapotranspiration, and internal processes controlling groundwater storage and discharge.

The most appropriate modelling approach depends on the **purpose of the model**, the **available observations**, the **current understanding of the system**, and the required **spatial and temporal resolution**.
