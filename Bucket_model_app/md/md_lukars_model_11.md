$Q_{\mathrm{is},i,t}$ ($m³/\Delta t$) is the **slow infiltration / groundwater recharge from hydrotope $i$ toward the matrix**.

The parameter $k_{\mathrm{is},i}$ controls the rate at which water stored in hydrotope $i$ is transferred toward the matrix through slow infiltration..


**Symbols in this equation**

| Symbol | Unit | Interpretation |
|---|---:|---|
| 🔵 $k_{\mathrm{is},i}$ | $1/\Delta t$ | Slow-infiltration coefficient controlling how rapidly water is transferred from hydrotope $i$ toward the matrix. |
| $E_{i,t}$ | mm | Water level (storage depth) in hydrotope $i$. |
| $A_i$ | m² | Area represented by hydrotope $i$. |
