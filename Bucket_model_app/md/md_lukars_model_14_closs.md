The **conduit drainage flux** $Q_{\mathrm{Closs},t}$ (m³/$\Delta t$) represents water that bypasses the modeled spring during high-flow conditions.

Drainage starts when the conduit water level $C_t$ exceeds the storage threshold $C_{\mathrm{loss}}$. The excess water is removed immediately from the system and the conduit water level is limited to $C_{\mathrm{loss}}$.

**Symbols in this equation**

| Symbol | Unit | Interpretation |
|---|---:|---|
| 🔵 $C_{\mathrm{loss}}$ | mm | Maximum conduit storage threshold. Water above this level is drained from the conduit and bypasses the modeled spring. |
| $R_a$ | m² | Total recharge area. |
| $\Delta t$ | time | Simulation time step. |
