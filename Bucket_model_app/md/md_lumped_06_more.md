Each **storage compartment (bucket)** represents a relevant part of the hydrological system, such as the soil, epikarst, fractured rock matrix, conduit network, or another effective storage domain. **Transfer functions** describe how water moves between these compartments and how it eventually contributes to spring discharge.

The compartments should not always be interpreted as exact physical volumes. They are simplified representations of the dominant processes required to reproduce the observed hydrological behaviour.
