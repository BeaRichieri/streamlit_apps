A fully lumped model treats the entire catchment as a **single spatial unit**.

Precipitation, evapotranspiration, storage, and discharge are represented as catchment-averaged quantities. Internal spatial differences are not represented explicitly, although several interconnected reservoirs may still be used to describe different hydrological processes.

**KarstMod** is an example of a modelling platform that can be used to construct fully lumped conceptual models of karst systems.
