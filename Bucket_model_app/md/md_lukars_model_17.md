| Parameter | Unit | Main role in the model |
|---|---:|---|
| $A_i$ | km² | Area represented by hydrotope $i$ |
| $k_{\mathrm{is},i}$ | $1/\Delta t$ | Rate of slow infiltration toward the matrix |
| $E_{\min,i}$ | mm | Lower threshold for deactivating rapid hydrotope flow |
| $E_{\max,i}$ | mm | Upper threshold for activating rapid hydrotope flow |
| $\alpha_i$ | – | Nonlinearity of the fast hydrotope-flow response |
| $k_{\mathrm{hyd},i}$ | mm²/$\Delta t$ | Scaling of fast hydrotope flow |
| $l_{\mathrm{hyd},i}$ | m | Mean distance of hydrotope $i$ from the spring |
| $k_{\mathrm{MC}}$ | mm/$\Delta t$ | Strength of matrix-conduit exchange |
| $a_{\mathrm{MC}}$ | – | Nonlinearity of matrix-conduit exchange |
| $k_{\mathrm{CS}}$ | mm/$\Delta t$ | Conduit drainage rate toward the spring |
| $a_{\mathrm{CS}}$ | – | Nonlinearity of conduit drainage |
| $k_{\mathrm{MS}}$ | mm/$\Delta t$ | Matrix drainage rate toward the spring |
| $a_{\mathrm{MS}}$ | – | Nonlinearity of matrix drainage |
