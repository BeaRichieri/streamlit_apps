Excessive complexity can lead to **overparameterization**, meaning that the available observations do not contain enough information to determine all model parameters reliably.

It can also lead to **overfitting**, where a model reproduces the calibration data very closely but performs poorly for independent periods or different hydrological conditions.

Several parameter combinations may sometimes produce similarly good simulations. This behaviour is commonly referred to as **equifinality** and is one reason why model evaluation should not rely on a single calibrated parameter set.
