By the end of the LuKARS exercises, participants should be able to:

- explain why different parts of a catchment may respond differently to the same precipitation event;
- describe the role of hydrotopes in a semi-distributed conceptual model;
- distinguish slow matrix discharge from rapid conduit discharge;
- interpret the main LuKARS storages and internal fluxes;
- relate selected model parameters to hydrograph peaks, recessions, and baseflow;
- perform a transparent manual calibration;
- evaluate a calibration using both numerical metrics and process-based evidence;
- recognise that different parameter combinations may produce similarly good simulations;
- discuss the implications of parameter uncertainty and equifinality.
